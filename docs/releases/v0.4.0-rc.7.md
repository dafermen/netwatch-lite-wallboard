## Delivery And Remaining Gates

Start with a clean reviewed commit. Use `scripts/publish-portable.ps1` to retain the matching extracted folder under `artifacts/portable/` and tested exact ZIP/SHA-256 under `artifacts/packages/`, refusing existing outputs. Push source only, wait for CI and use `scripts/publish-prerelease.ps1 -Mode Draft` to upload those same two local assets. Download and compare both files, recheck draft/source/asset identities and verify old versions remain unchanged. Ordinary CI may generate a separate validation artifact; it is not the draft's binary.

Keep operator JSON/local pages at the new root and `app/` intact. Do not overwrite working deployments or mix runtime files. Browser sessions stay in their existing LocalAppData location. Manual packaged visual/taskbar/DPI, real company-page, other-device and long-session production acceptance remain required before public/stable approval. This draft does not silently waive them.
