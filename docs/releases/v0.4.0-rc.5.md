## Recovery And Limits

Protection is off in old/examples JSON, does not kill processes or delete browser data and cannot guarantee Windows never runs out of resources. UI profile selection is session-only and never rewrites JSON. Sanitized export excludes raw diagnostics/logs; Copy remains raw. The first legacy-log rotation discards history beyond its bounded tail. Published/draft assets must never be replaced under the same version; failures leave a draft for inspection, not automatic repair.
